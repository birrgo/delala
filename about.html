Our Story

Hulum Gebeya (which translates to "All Market") was born out of a simple vision: to create a unified, digital ecosystem where people can find absolutely anything they need. Whether you are looking to buy a new phone, rent an apartment, or hire a certified plumber, we believed it should all be accessible from a single, trusted application.

Today, Hulum Gebeya is bridging the gap between convenience and reliability, providing a powerful platform that connects thousands of buyers, sellers, landlords, and service providers every day.
