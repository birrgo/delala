Data Deletion Policy

At Hulum Gebeya, we value your privacy. If you wish to delete your account or any personal data (such as listings, profile information, or chat history) associated with our app, you have the right to request its complete removal.

How to Delete Your Data Inside the App

If you currently have the app installed, you can delete your data directly:

  • Open the Hulum Gebeya app.
  • Navigate to your Profile or Settings.
  • Select your listing/post and click "Delete", OR choose the "Delete Account" option to permanently remove all your data.

How to Request Data Deletion via Email

If you no longer have the app installed and wish to have your data deleted, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support team at the address below.
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address or username associated with your Hulum Gebeya account.

📩 your-email@example.com

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving your request, we will delete the following within 7-14 business days:

  • Your profile information (Name, Email, Phone number).
  • All your active and inactive marketplace listings and photos.
  • Your direct messaging (chat) history.

Note: For security and legal purposes, standard server logs may be retained for up to 90 days before being automatically and permanently deleted.
